# Privacy

CalorieTracker AI has no server and no account. This is what happens to your data.

## Stays on your device
- Your meals, weight history, water and creatine log, dietitian memories, profile and targets (local Room database and app preferences).
- Your API keys, encrypted with a key held by the Android Keystore.
- Exported JSON and CSV files, saved in the app's documents folder when you choose to export.

Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for this app.

## Sent to the AI provider you choose
When you log a meal, chat with the dietitian, or a coaching notification is generated, the app sends to **your selected provider** (Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic or DeepSeek), using **your** key:

- the text you typed and any photo you attached in chat;
- context that helps the answer: today's and yesterday's meals and totals, a short weekly summary, your targets, cuisine and diet type, weight trend, and the memories the dietitian holds.

How long that provider keeps this data is set by their terms, not by this app.

## Not collected
No analytics, advertising identifiers, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.

## Your controls
Delete a meal (with undo), edit or delete any memory, clear a key by emptying its field, export your data, or uninstall the app to remove everything stored on the device.
